## Privacy Policy for [SERVICE NAME]

This Privacy Policy describes how [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY] collects, uses and deletes information through [SERVICE NAME] (the "Service"). The Service automatically sends a direct message to Instagram users who comment on posts of the Instagram professional account [@your_account].

### 1. Information we collect
When a user comments on a post of [@your_account] or sends a message to [@your_account], we receive the following from Meta through the Instagram API:
- Instagram-scoped user ID and username
- Comment text, comment ID and the ID of the commented post
- Messages and button selections sent to [@your_account]
- Whether the user follows [@your_account]

We do not collect passwords, email addresses, phone numbers or payment information.

### 2. How we use the information
Only to detect comments that contain a keyword, send the requested link by direct message, reply to the comment, and check whether the user follows [@your_account] before sending the link. We do not use it for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell or share it.

### 3. Data retention
Comment and message data is processed when received and not stored permanently. A delivery log (comment or message ID and time) is kept for up to 30 days to prevent duplicate messages and then deleted automatically.

### 4. Data deletion
Email [your@email.com] with your Instagram username and the subject "Data deletion request". We delete all related data within 30 days and confirm by email. You can also stop all processing by deleting your comment or blocking [@your_account].
